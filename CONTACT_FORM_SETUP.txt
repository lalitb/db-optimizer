# Contact Form Setup Guide

## ✅ What's Been Added

Your landing page now has a comprehensive contact form with:
- **Name** field
- **Email** field  
- **Company** field
- **Message** textarea (for use case details)
- **Newsletter opt-in** checkbox
- **Success/Error messaging**
- **Loading states**

## 🔧 Setup Instructions (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Get Your Free Web3Forms Access Key

1. Go to https://web3forms.com
2. Click "Get Started Free"
3. Enter your email address (where you want to receive form submissions)
4. You'll receive an **Access Key** via email

### Step 2: Add Your Access Key to the Landing Page

1. Open `/Users/lalitb/personal/db-optimizer/landing-page/index.html`
2. Find line ~1433 (search for `YOUR_ACCESS_KEY_HERE`)
3. Replace `YOUR_ACCESS_KEY_HERE` with your actual access key

```html
<!-- BEFORE -->
<input type="hidden" name="access_key" value="YOUR_ACCESS_KEY_HERE">

<!-- AFTER (example) -->
<input type="hidden" name="access_key" value="a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7890-abcd-ef1234567890">
```

### Step 3: Test the Form

1. Open `landing-page/index.html` in your browser
2. Scroll to the "Request Beta Access" section
3. Fill out the form with test data
4. Click "Request Beta Access"
5. You should see: ✅ "Thank you! We'll be in touch within 24 hours."
6. Check your email - you'll receive the form submission!

## 🎨 Form Features

### Fields Collected:
- **Full Name** (required)
- **Work Email** (required)
- **Company Name** (required)
- **Use Case Details** (required) - Helps you qualify leads
- **Newsletter Opt-in** (optional)

### User Experience:
- **Loading state**: Button shows "Sending..." while submitting
- **Success message**: Green confirmation with checkmark
- **Error handling**: Red error message with fallback email
- **Form reset**: Clears after successful submission
- **Responsive**: Works on mobile and desktop

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

- **No database**: Form data goes directly to your email
- **No tracking**: Web3Forms doesn't store submissions
- **GDPR compliant**: No cookies, no tracking scripts
- **Free tier**: 250 submissions/month
